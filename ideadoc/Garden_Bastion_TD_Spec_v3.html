3.3 Root Animation ANIM

  • Root breathing: each root scales 1 ± 0.03 along its length, phase offset by root index, period 3.2 s.
  • Attack grip: on heavy attacks (Pineapple, Golem, Flytrap) roots dig in: shorten 8% for 0.1 s, then spring back (k = 320, c = 20).
  • Stun: roots go limp (droop 15°) and veins dim to 20% until the stun ends.
  • Build roots with a shared tapered TubeGeometry per root shape (6 shapes, built once at startup), never per tower.

4.2 Upgrade v3

Keep the v2 upgrade moment (§6.2) and add:

  • Root surge: a light pulse travels from the root tips up the stem in 0.2 s, then the model swap happens behind the white flash.
  • Ground growth: new stones, moss or flowers for the new level grow in with a 0.3 s spring, not a sudden swap.
  • Lv.5: roots spread outward in a ring; the rune circle draws itself clockwise in 0.6 s; light pillar as in v2.

4.3 Sell — “Return to Soil” (0.6 s)

  • Plant wilts (stem bends 40°, leaves droop), then shrinks into the mound.
  • Roots pull back into the center one by one.
  • Mound flattens; a leaf swirl (20) rises; +$ coins fly to the Cash counter.
  • The grass grows back over the spot during the next 2 s.

9.1 Boss Animation Rules

  • Each boss has an idle loop, a walk cycle, a roar (used at entrance and each phase change), and one attack anim per phase.
  • Every boss attack has a 1.2 s+ telegraph: glow on the body part that will attack, plus a red ground ring.
  • Stagger meter: a thin yellow bar under the boss HP. Big hits fill it. When full, boss is stunned 2 s, kneels, and takes +25% damage. Shows a “STAGGERED!” banner.
  • Boss finale (v2 §8.8) plays for every boss, with its own color (Hydra green, Queen orange, Empress ice-blue).

13. Meta Progression v3 NEW

13.1 Living Garden Hub

  • The lobby diorama shows every unit you own planted in a small hub garden. New pulls grow in with the §4.1 seed animation the next time you return to the lobby.
  • Click a plant in the hub to open its Inventory page. Godly/Exclusive plants stand on a raised stone stage.

  • 30 tiers, earned by playing (XP from waves, quests and bosses). Resets every 6 weeks.
  • Rewards: Seeds, Dewdrops, soil skins, new titles, one Mythical unit at tier 30.

17. Audio v3 UPGRADE

  • Per-map music key: Backyard C major, Jungle D Dorian, Volcano E Phrygian, Frosty A minor with bell layer.

  • Separate volume sliders: Music, SFX, UI.
